02 · Solution
A third presence in the room — the Co-Teacher
🗣 Real-time voice, in the lesson
Browser mic → live transcript → multi-agent AI. When a child asks a question, Sahayak answers out loud — in the teacher's flow, never interrupting their moment.
🔉 Private whisper to one child
"Priya, don't worry — when adding halves and quarters, find a common denominator." Whispered only to her. Zero embarrassment, instant clarity.
🧠 Live confusion radar
"12 of 30 students are confused about common denominators — right now." Sahayak clusters the same question across students and tells the teacher mid-lesson.
🌀 Floor manager
An AI that knows when to speak and when to stay quiet — respecting turn-taking so it amplifies the teacher instead of competing with them.
9 agents working together
Lesson Context · Replier · Quiz · Whisper · Confusion Clustering · Insights · Floor Manager · TTS · Orchestrator
Teacher stays in control
Mute anytime. AI never replaces the teacher — it holds the second mic.
Zero-device classroom
Runs on the teacher's laptop + student phones/mics already in the room. No new hardware wall.
Every student gets a personal tutor. The teacher gets superpowers.
